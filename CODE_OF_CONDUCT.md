# Community standards

Treat contributors and operators respectfully. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Use constructive feedback and respect confidentiality.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing private operational data are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close discussions when necessary. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using the contact details they provide on the hosting platform. Do not invent or assume an official reporting address.
